Why is my h1 hidden by my navbar?


Wednesday 2:18 PM
Your navbar uses fixed, so it is removed from the normal document flow. The page content starts at the very top and sits underneath it.
Add top padding to the content:
export default function HomePage() {
  return (
    <main className="min-h-screen pt-24 flex items-start justify-center">
      <div className="space-y-3 max-w-3xl">
        <h1 className="text-5xl font-semibold">Peer Tutoring Hub</h1>

        <Image
          src="/images/tutoring.png"
          alt="Student meeting with a peer tutor"
          loading="eager"
          width={500}
          height={500}
        />
      </div>
    </main>
  );
}
Or apply it globally in your layout:
<body className="min-h-full flex flex-col">
  <Navbar />
  <main className="pt-24">{children}</main>
</body>
pt-24 provides space below the fixed navbar. Also, your navbar has a typo: transistion should be transition.

can I just remove fixed?


Wednesday 2:19 PM
Yes. Removing fixed lets the navbar remain in the normal document flow, so the page content will appear below it automatically:
<nav className="w-full flex items-center justify-around py-5 px-24 border-b border-gray-700 bg-gray-700">
Your <h1> should then no longer be hidden.
